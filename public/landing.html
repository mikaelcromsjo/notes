Notes

Your notes as a living map. Link them together, tap to move between them, and never lose track of what's due — online or off.

Open the app View on GitHub

Navigate by connection, not by folder

Every note sits at the center of its own neighborhood — the notes you've linked to it, or moved between most. Tap a neighbor to step there. No folder tree to maintain, no tags to remember: your notes stay organized by how they actually connect.

Works offline, syncs when you're back

Install it like a native app. Read, write, and link notes with no connection at all — every change queues up and replays automatically the moment you're back online, with no lost edits.

Ranked by how you actually use it

The grid around a note isn't alphabetical or chronological — neighbors are ranked by navigation history, so the links you follow most often surface first. New notes fall back to link recency. The graph reflects how you think, not how you filed things.
